LINE COOK / BUTCHER, PREP COOK, SAUTE AND GRILL
**Line Cook / Butcher – Prep Cook, Sauté & Grill (Hot Line)**  

**Reports to:** Kitchen Manager **Employment type:** Non‑exempt (hourly)  

---

### Position Summary  
The line cook is responsible for accurately and efficiently cooking meats, fish, vegetables, soups, and other hot‑food items, as well as preparing and portioning foods before cooking. The role also includes plating, garnishing, and preparing appropriate garnishes for all hot‑menu items [1].

---

### Core Duties & Responsibilities  

| Area | Key Tasks |
|------|-----------|
| **Food Preparation** | • Prepare a variety of meats, seafood, poultry, vegetables, and other items for broiling, grilling, frying, sautéing, or other cooking methods (portioning, battering, breading, seasoning, marinating).<br>• Portion foods according to standard portion sizes and recipe specifications.<br>• Assist with food‑prep assignments during off‑peak periods. [1][2] |
| **Cooking & Quality** | • Operate broilers, ovens, grills, fryers, sauté burners, flat‑top ranges, convection ovens, pasta cookers, etc.<br>• Assume 100 % responsibility for the quality of products served and consistently follow standard cooking methods, portion sizes, and quality standards. [1] |
| **Station Management** | • Stock and maintain sufficient food levels at line stations to ensure smooth service.<br>• Keep workstations, tables, shelves, grills, and equipment clean and sanitary.<br>• Handle, store, and rotate all products properly. [1][2] |
| **Plating & Presentation** | • Follow proper plate presentation and garnish set‑up for all dishes.<br>• Prepare appropriate garnishes for hot‑menu plates. [1][2] |
| **Closing & Miscellaneous** | • Close the kitchen properly using the closing checklist.<br>• Attend scheduled employee meetings and suggest improvements.<br>• Perform other duties as assigned by the Kitchen Manager or manager‑on‑duty. [3] |

---

### Required Qualifications  

* Minimum **2 years** kitchen preparation and cooking experience (at least **6 months** in a similar capacity). [3]  
* Ability to communicate clearly with managers, kitchen, and dining‑room personnel. [3]  
* Physical ability to reach, bend, stoop, and lift up to **40 lb**, and to stand for long periods (up to 9 hours). [3]  
* Ability to follow printed recipes, plate specifications, and maintain personal hygiene per Houston Health Department standards. [3]  

---

**Note:** All information is drawn from the internal job‑description document “54_Line‑Cook.pdf” [1‑3].
